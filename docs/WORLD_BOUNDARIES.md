# World Boundaries — Merge

## World size

- **Total world area:** 40 km²
- **Reference dimensions:** 6.32 × 6.32 km
- The three kingdoms are contained within this total area.

## Outer boundaries

The playable world has **no invisible wall** and no artificial barrier at its outer limits.

The external boundaries are part of the natural geography of the world:

- **Ocean:** the **eastern outer boundary** is formed by a large ocean that cannot be crossed within the scope of the game.
- **Mountains:** the remaining outer limits are formed by high, steep and naturally impassable mountain ranges.

These boundaries must be presented as genuine geographic features rather than technical level limits. The player should understand that the terrain and environment prevent further travel, not an invisible gameplay barrier.

## Design rule

The player must never encounter an invisible wall at the edge of the world.

The ocean and mountain ranges should visually and physically communicate the end of the accessible landmass while suggesting that the wider world continues beyond the playable 40 km².

## Internal borders

The natural outer boundaries described here are distinct from the borders between the three kingdoms. Internal kingdom borders may remain traversable and are to be defined separately through geography, politics, settlements, roads, guarded passages, natural terrain and other world-design elements.
